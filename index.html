T.I.D.A.L
Case

TIDAL Case is designed to assist educators and enhance Grade 8 students' understanding of Tides using a hands-on model that demonstrates the relationship between the Earth and lunar tides. Designed as a compact and portable device, this simulation relieves teachers of the burden of hauling bulky materials while ensuring lasting durability.

Process

How to Use

01

Place the suitcase on a flat, stable table. Open the suitcase carefully. Observe the model. Look for the position of Earth, Moon, Sun, and tidal bulges and check if all components are properly attached. Make sure that the rotating platform is working and secured. Turn the Earth slowly from west to east.Observe the movement of the coastal location. Stop at the first tidal bulge and record the observation. Continue rotating the Earth, stop when the coastal location is between the tidal bulges. Observe the change in the condition of the tide.

02

Rotate the earth until the coastal location is opposite the Moon and observe the tidal bulge. Compare the observation with the first position and explain why the opposite side also experiences high tide.

03

Stop rotating the earth. Return the moon to its original position. Check if all parts are secure. Close the suitcase carefully.

HOW IT WORKS

New Moon — Spring Tide

The Moon is between the Sun and Earth, so their tidal effects reinforce each other.

What is happening?

Because the Sun, Moon, and Earth are nearly in a straight line, the Moon’s tidal effect and the Sun’s tidal effect work together. This makes the difference between high tide and low tide larger. That larger tidal range is called a spring tide.
