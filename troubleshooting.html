01 — Troubleshoot

Something
went wrong?

Use this page to identify common problems with the tool and find the appropriate solution or maintenance procedure.

Tool won't turn on

Check that the device is properly connected to its power source. Make sure the power switch is turned on and inspect the cable or connection for visible damage.

Power

Tool produces unusual results

Check the settings and make sure the tool is being used according to the recommended procedure. Repeat the process if necessary.

Operation

Tool behaves unusually

Stop using the tool and check for loose, damaged, or incorrectly positioned parts. Do not continue operating the device if the problem persists.

Warning

Component appears damaged

Do not use the damaged component. Follow the recommended maintenance procedure or contact the appropriate person for repair or replacement.

Damage

Problem still persists

Stop using the device and refer to the official maintenance instructions or contact the person responsible for servicing the equipment.

Service
Maintenance

Don't make the problem worse.

If the problem cannot be resolved using the troubleshooting steps above, stop operating the device. Refer to the manufacturer's maintenance instructions or contact the appropriate person responsible for servicing the equipment.
